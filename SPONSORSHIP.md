# Sponsoring Violin

Violin is an open-source project maintained by Strategic Automation Ltd.
Sponsorship helps sustain the ongoing work behind the project. Violin remains
[MIT licensed](LICENSE), and using or contributing to it does not require a
sponsorship.

This document is the public source of truth for Violin's sponsorship policy.
The [website sponsorship page](https://strategic-automation.github.io/violin/support.html)
provides a shorter introduction.

## Why sponsor?

Maintenance continues between visible releases. Sponsorship helps make time
for the work that keeps Violin documented, tested, and maintained:

- Maintenance and compatibility work.
- Testing and verification.
- Documentation and onboarding.
- Website upkeep.
- Issue triage and community/reviewer support.
- Preparing dependable releases.

Contributions support this shared work; they are not payments for a particular
issue, feature, or delivery date.

## Ways to support Violin

[Sponsor Violin on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/Strategic-Automation).
Payments are handled through GitHub Sponsors.

These are the programme's working options. Check GitHub Sponsors for the
options currently available before making a contribution.

| Option | Working amount | Support and recognition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Violin Supporter | $5/month | Sponsor updates and optional public acknowledgement |
| Violin Sustainer | $25/month | Sponsor updates and an optional name listing on the sponsorship page |
| Organisation Supporter | $100/month | Sponsor updates and an optional organisation name, approved logo, and main website link |
| Custom amount | You choose | General thanks and recognition agreed with you |
| One-time support | You choose | A non-recurring way to support maintenance |

All options support the project. They are support and recognition tiers,
not support contracts or purchases of engineering priority. Recognition is
always optional; a larger contribution does not give a sponsor more control.

## What sponsorship does not provide

Sponsorship does not include:

- Guaranteed support, a support SLA, response times, or issue resolution.
- Feature priority, roadmap voting, product control, or delivery commitments.
- Consulting hours or penetration-testing services.
- Sponsor-only product features or private vulnerability information.
- Influence over security policy, security-policy exceptions, or special
  permissions to use or test systems.

The existing contribution and security processes apply to everyone. Use
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for issues and contributions and
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private vulnerability reporting.

## Recognition and privacy

Project-managed recognition is opt-in. Sponsors may remain private and
receive the same thanks without a public listing. We do not infer permission
to list someone from their sponsorship or their GitHub visibility setting.

- Individuals are listed only with permission, using their chosen name or
  GitHub username.
- Organisations may opt into a name, an approved logo, and a main website
  link. We agree the displayed details with the organisation before adding
  them.
- Sponsor email addresses and payment amounts are never displayed in
  project-managed recognition. Listings are not grouped by tier, so recognition
  does not reveal a sponsor's contribution level.
- Recognition is managed manually at launch. There is no automatic sponsor
  import, webhook, or publication of sponsor details.
- Sponsors can contact Strategic Automation to change or remove their
  recognition. GitHub's own sponsorship visibility is managed separately
  through GitHub.

An acknowledgement means thanks for supporting maintenance. It does not
imply project control, security review, blanket endorsement of either party,
or special permissions.

### Organisation logos and links

Organisation recognition uses only a name, logo, and main website link
approved by that organisation and reviewed by the maintainers. Supply only
material you have permission to share. A listing is an acknowledgement, not
an advertisement, certification, or endorsement. We do not display payment
amounts or rank organisations by contribution.

## Questions and larger organisations

For sponsorship questions, recognition preferences, or a larger contribution,
use the public contact details on
[Strategic Automation's organisation profile](https://github.com/Strategic-Automation).
There is no requirement to sponsor before asking a question. This contact
route does not make sponsorship a purchase of consultancy or other services.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to sponsor to use Violin?

No. Violin remains MIT licensed. Sponsorship does not change the licence or
create sponsor-only product features.

### Will sponsorship get my issue or feature prioritised?

No. Sponsorship does not buy feature priority, guaranteed issue resolution,
or a support SLA. Use the usual contribution channels for requests.

### Can I sponsor privately?

Yes. Public recognition requires your permission. We never publish sponsor
email addresses or payment amounts in project acknowledgements. Choose your
preferred visibility separately on GitHub Sponsors.

### Can my organisation be recognised?

Yes, if you opt in. We agree the name, approved logo, and main website link
before a manual listing. Recognition does not imply project control,
security review, endorsement, or special permissions.

### Can I give a custom amount or support the project once?

Custom and one-time support are part of the working options above. Visit
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Strategic-Automation) to confirm
the available options and choose an amount that works for you.

### Can sponsorship pay for a penetration test or private security advice?

No. Sponsorship supports the open-source project. It does not include
penetration-testing services, consulting hours, private vulnerability
information, or exceptions to security policy.

### Can I help without giving money?

Yes. Clear issue reports, documentation improvements, reviews, and other
contributions are welcome through the existing
[contribution process](CONTRIBUTING.md).
