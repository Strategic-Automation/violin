Support Violin

Help sustain Violin.

Violin is an open-source pentest profile for supervised, authorized assessments with Hermes Agent. It remains MIT licensed. Sponsorship helps sustain the ongoing work behind the project.

Payments are handled through GitHub Sponsors. The sponsorship policy and FAQ are the public source of truth.

What your support sustains

Open-source maintenance takes time beyond writing code. Sponsorship helps Strategic Automation maintain Violin and care for the work that keeps it useful to its community:

  • Maintenance, compatibility work, testing, and verification.
  • Documentation, onboarding, and website upkeep.
  • Issue triage, community and reviewer support, and dependable releases.

Violin remains available under the MIT license. You do not need to sponsor to use it or contribute.

What sponsorship does not provide

Sponsorship does not include a support SLA, guaranteed support or issue resolution, feature priority, roadmap votes, or control of the project. It does not buy consulting hours, penetration-testing services, sponsor-only features, private vulnerability information, or exceptions to the security policy.

Larger organisations can contact Strategic Automation through its public organisation profile to discuss sponsorship or recognition. Any services enquiry is separate from sponsorship and carries no commitment to provide services.

Recognition is your choice

You may remain private. Public recognition is opt-in and managed manually: we ask permission before listing an individual, using their chosen name or GitHub username. We never display sponsors' email addresses or payment amounts, or group names by tier.

Organisations may opt into a name, a logo they approve and are entitled to share, and a main website link. Maintainers review these before publication. You can request a change or removal through the organisation contact route.

Recognition acknowledges support. It does not imply project control, a security review, endorsement, or special permissions.
